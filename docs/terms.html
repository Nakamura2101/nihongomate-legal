BamenCue offers a free trial and a paid subscription. The free trial gives you three selected lessons for seven days. The seven days begin when you first speak in a lesson and a practice attempt is recorded — opening the app or browsing lessons does not start them.

The free trial is available only once to each eligible user. When the seven days are over it does not start again, and those three lessons lock together with the rest of the catalog.

BamenCue Premium unlocks every published lesson. It is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. You choose a monthly plan or a yearly plan, at the price shown in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, and the subscription renews automatically for the same plan unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

You manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings, not in the app. Deleting your BamenCue account does not cancel it. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store terms that apply to you.

Starting Premium uses up the free trial. If your subscription later ends, the free trial does not come back and every lesson stays locked until you subscribe again.
