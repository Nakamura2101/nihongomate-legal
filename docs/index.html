BamenCue

BamenCue is a scenario-based Japanese speaking-practice app. You practise everyday conversations out loud, and the app assesses the pronunciation of what you say.

It is a learning aid — not a translation service, not a certification, and not a government, medical or legal service.

You can use BamenCue without an account. Signing in is optional, and practice never requires it.
