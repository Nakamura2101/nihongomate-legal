Privacy Policy

Version 6 · Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Who we are

BamenCue is a Japanese speaking-practice app operated by Naoki Nakamura ("we", "us").

BamenCue is a learning app. It is not a government, medical, legal or other official service, and it does not give legal, immigration, medical or other professional advice.

If you have a question about this policy, contact us at bamencue@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy describes how the BamenCue mobile app handles your information. It does not cover services that other companies operate, even when the app connects to them.

3. Information we handle

The app handles the following:

  • Settings and preferences — the choices you make in the app.
  • Profile information — what you enter during first-run setup, such as a display name, an avatar and your self-reported Japanese level. You may also state a gender; that is optional, "not stated" is a valid answer, and practice never requires it.
  • Practice history — a record of your practice activity, including the transcripts of what you said, how long you practised, and the audio the app played in the conversation.

Your settings and preferences are stored on your device and are not synchronized as preferences to another device. The language, voice profile, support tier and practice mode used in a session may be saved as part of your account's practice history. If you sign in, your profile information may be saved to your account so you can use it again (section 7), and your practice history is saved as described in section 9.

4. Microphone and audio

Speaking practice needs the microphone. Your device asks for microphone permission the first time you hold the speak button, and the app records only while you hold that button.

When you use speaking practice, audio may be processed to provide speaking-practice features, including pronunciation feedback. This may involve service providers, as described in section 8.

How long voice recordings are kept is described in section 6.

5. Recordings kept on your device

Some recordings may be kept on your own device so you can play them back during practice or review. They stay on your device: the app does not delete them automatically and does not currently offer an in-app action to delete them. Uninstalling the app removes them, as does clearing the app's storage in your device settings where your device offers that.

6. Retention of voice recordings

We do not retain your voice recordings after processing, except for recordings kept on your device for playback or review as described in section 5, and — only if voice backup is available and you turn it on — the recordings saved to your account as described in section 9. Audio sent for pronunciation processing is handled by the service providers described in section 8 under their own terms; we do not keep it after the request.

7. Optional account

You can use BamenCue without an account. Signing in is entirely optional.

If you choose to sign in, you can do so through a third-party sign-in provider, or with a one-time sign-in link sent to your email address. Your email address is held by your chosen sign-in provider as your sign-in identity.

Signing in creates an account for you. If you sign in, profile information associated with your account may be saved so it is available to you. You can change it later in Profile.

The email address on your account is shown to you in Profile.

8. Service providers

We may use service providers where necessary to provide BamenCue. Where information is shared with them, we share only what is needed for that purpose and require appropriate protection, consistent with this policy.

The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

If you buy a subscription, Apple processes the payment: we do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple provides signed transaction information about your subscription, and the app may send it to our server, where it is used only to check whether your Premium access is active.

9. Saving and restoring your practice history

When you are signed in, your practice history is saved to your BamenCue account automatically. This covers the transcript text of what you said and the audio the app played in the conversation, so that you can review a past session after reinstalling the app or changing device. It happens in the background after a session ends, when you sign in, and when you return to the app. You do not need to tap anything, and Profile also offers a manual backup if you want to start it yourself.

It may not finish straight away. If your device is offline or a request does not succeed, the app keeps that session and tries again later, and until it succeeds that session is saved only on your device. Saving to your account does not remove the practice history on your device, and you can also restore it to a device yourself from Profile.

Practice you do without signing in stays on your device. Those sessions are not saved to your account, and signing in later does not upload them — not automatically and not through the manual backup. Only sessions you practise while signed in are saved to your account.

We use your practice history to provide the app's own learning features: Session Review, the summary after a session, your progress, and the lists that show what you have practised. We do not sell it, we do not share it for advertising or marketing, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Voice backup is separate and optional, and it is off unless you turn it on. It is offered only when we have enabled the feature; while it is not enabled, recordings of your voice are not saved to your account at all and the app does not show the setting. When it is available and you turn it on, recordings of your own speech are saved to your account so you can play them back after reinstalling the app or changing device. These recordings can be accessed only through your signed-in account; we do not make them available to other users or to third parties for their own purposes, and we do not use them for advertising or to train AI models. We and our service providers can access them only as needed to run, secure and support the service.

You can turn voice backup off at any time. We then stop saving recordings and delete the recordings already saved to your account; if a stored file cannot be removed immediately, section 11 applies. Your practice history and the audio the app played are not affected, and the recordings on your device stay until you remove them.

10. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising or marketing. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not use your practice history or your voice recordings to train AI models. We do not require your name, gender or level in order to let you practise.

11. Keeping and deleting your information

On your device: your preferences, practice history and recordings kept on your device remain until you remove them. The app does not currently offer an in-app "delete all local data" action. Uninstalling the app removes the learning data it stored on your device.

Deleting your account: if you have signed in, "Delete account data" in Profile deletes your BamenCue account and the data associated with it, including the practice history, the audio and any voice recordings saved to your account. The app also signs you out and removes your account's profile on this device.

What this does not delete:

  • practice history, preferences and recordings stored on this device (uninstalling the app removes them);
  • your account with your sign-in provider (Apple, Google or email). If you signed in with Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is also withdrawn; for other providers, you can manage that access in their account settings;
  • an Apple subscription is not cancelled automatically. Cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.

We have not set a fixed retention period. We keep account data until you delete it or until it is no longer needed for the purposes described here. If some stored files cannot be removed immediately, we keep only a minimal reference to them until their removal is completed.

When data is deleted from your account, copies can remain for a limited period in protected backup and recovery storage used to guard against loss and accidental deletion, and in our providers' own recovery systems. These copies are not used to provide the app; they are overwritten or removed as their retention periods expire.

You can also ask us to delete your account data without using the app. Email bamencue@gmail.com and we will delete it. You do not need to reinstall the app to make the request. The same address is for any other question or request about your information. See also Privacy choices.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, and use secure connections when information is transmitted.

No app, service or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

BamenCue is intended for general audiences learning Japanese. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from a child in a way that would require a parent's consent. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at bamencue@gmail.com and we will delete it.

14. Languages

This policy is written in English, which is the authoritative version. Translations may be provided for other languages the app supports. If a translation and the English version disagree, the English version applies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The version and effective date at the top of this document change with it. Continuing to use the app after an update means the updated policy applies to you.
