Support

Check the common questions below, or email the developer directly. The same help is available inside the app under Settings → Support & Information → Help.

Common help

The microphone is not working

Speaking practice needs microphone permission. If the microphone is unavailable, open your device settings for NihongoMate, allow microphone access, then return to the lesson and try again.

Sign-in or connection problems

Signing in is optional and you can keep practising without an account. If a sign-in or a practice request fails, check your internet connection and try again. What you have already practised on this device is not lost.

History and Progress are empty

History and Progress appear after you complete practice sessions.

Deleting your account data

In the app, under Account & security, "Delete account data" removes the data associated with your NihongoMate account and signs you out. You can also request deletion by email without using the app. The sign-in account you use with your sign-in provider is not deleted, and the practice history on this device stays. See Privacy choices for both routes and the full detail.
